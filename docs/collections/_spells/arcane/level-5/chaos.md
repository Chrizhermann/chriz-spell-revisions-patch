Chaos  
Level: 5  
School: Enchantment  
Range: Long  
Duration: 4 rounds (5 at 15th level, 6 at 20th level)  
Casting Time: 4  
Area of Effect: 30' radius  
Saving Throw: Spell negates  
  
This spell works like the 4th-level spell Confusion, but it is harder to resist. All hostile creatures within the area of effect are allowed a saving throw vs. spell at -4. Those successfully saving are unaffected by the spell. Those who fail their saving throws will either go berserk, stand confused, or wander about for the duration of the spell. Wandering creatures move as far from the caster as possible, according to their most typical mode of movement (characters walk, fish swim, bats fly, etc.). Any confused creature that is attacked perceives the attacker as an enemy and acts according to its basic nature.
